Activities

Beach Volleyball & Frisbee

Embrace the sun and soft breeze with friendly competition right on Dahican's powdery white sand. The resort offers dedicated spaces for Beach Volleyball and Frisbee, perfect for families, friends, or spontaneous games with fellow guests.

Crystal Kayak & Paddle Board

Discover the crystal-clear waters of Dahican in the most immersive way possible with the resort's Crystal Kayaks and Paddle Boards. These transparent watercrafts offer a unique window into the vibrant marine life below, letting you glide over coral gardens and tropical fish in total awe.

Fitness Amenities

These amenities are part of the resort's commitment to promoting a holistic approach to health and well-being, set against the backdrop of Dahican's natural beauty. Whether you're starting your day with sunrise yoga or winding down with a sunset jog along the shore, every experience is designed to nourish both body and mind.
